================================================================================
                        FET WEB - PRIVACY POLICY
================================================================================

Last Updated: 2024

1. INTRODUCTION
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
FET Web ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This
Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information when you use our
educational timetabling web application.

2. DATA COLLECTION & STORAGE
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
FET Web operates as a client-side application. All data processing occurs
locally in your web browser using IndexedDB storage.

We DO NOT:
- Collect personal information
- Store data on remote servers
- Use tracking cookies
- Share data with third parties
- Perform analytics on your usage

We DO:
- Store timetable data locally in your browser
- Allow you to import/export your own data files
- Process all computations on your device

3. LOCAL DATA STORAGE
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Your timetable data, including:
- Teachers, subjects, and student groups
- Activities and scheduling constraints
- Room configurations
- Generated timetables

...is stored exclusively in your browser's IndexedDB. This data:
- Never leaves your device unless you explicitly export it
- Can be cleared at any time using browser settings or the app's reset feature
- Is not accessible to us or any third party

4. DATA EXPORT
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
When you export data (e.g., downloading timetables), files are generated
locally and saved directly to your device. No data transmission occurs.

5. THIRD-PARTY SERVICES
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
FET Web may use content delivery networks (CDNs) to serve static assets.
These services may log access requests per their own privacy policies.

6. COOKIES
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
FET Web does not use cookies for tracking or advertising purposes.
Essential browser storage (IndexedDB, localStorage) is used solely for
application functionality.

7. CHILDREN'S PRIVACY
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
FET Web is designed for educational institutions and administrators.
We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

8. YOUR RIGHTS
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
You have complete control over your data:
- View: All data is accessible through the application interface
- Export: Download your data at any time
- Delete: Clear all data using the Reset function or browser settings
- Portability: Export and import data in standard formats

9. CHANGES TO THIS POLICY
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Changes will be reflected
in the "Last Updated" date above.

10. CONTACT
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
FET Web is open-source software based on FET (Free Educational Timetabling).
For questions about this privacy policy, please visit the project repository.
