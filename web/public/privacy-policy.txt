================================================================================
                        FET WEB - PRIVACY POLICY
================================================================================

Last Updated: 2026-08-30

1. INTRODUCTION
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
FET Web ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This
Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information when you use our
educational timetabling web application.

2. DATA COLLECTION & STORAGE
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The application is local-first. Without signing in, timetable data is stored
in IndexedDB in your browser and scheduling computations run on your device.

If you choose Google sign-in, we receive the account identifier, email,
display name, and profile photo supplied by Google Authentication. Your named
schools, academic-year workspaces, and encrypted-in-transit timetable snapshot
files are stored in private Firebase Firestore and Cloud Storage paths assigned
to your account. Both personal Google accounts and Google Workspace accounts
may be used.

Optional usage analytics are disabled until you explicitly consent. When
enabled, Firebase Analytics receives technical events such as page routes,
generation duration, import type, and export format. Event parameters are
filtered to exclude names, email addresses, user IDs, file names, school data,
teacher data, and timetable contents. Advertising storage and ad
personalisation remain disabled.

3. LOCAL DATA STORAGE
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Your timetable data, including:
- Teachers, subjects, and student groups
- Activities and scheduling constraints
- Room configurations
- Generated timetables

...is stored in your browser's IndexedDB. For signed-in cloud workspaces, a
snapshot is also uploaded to Firebase so it can be restored on your other
devices. Each signed-in user can access only their own Firebase paths under
the application's security rules.

Local data can be cleared from the app or browser settings. Signing out clears
signed-in workspace data from the local browser. Account deletion removes the
user's Firestore documents, Storage snapshots, and Firebase Authentication
identity through a server-side deletion operation.

4. DATA EXPORT
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
When you export data (e.g., downloading timetables), files are generated
locally and saved directly to your device. No data transmission occurs.

5. THIRD-PARTY SERVICES
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The hosted app uses Google Firebase for Authentication, Firestore, Cloud
Storage, Hosting, Cloud Functions, App Check, and (only after consent)
Analytics. Google processes related technical data according to its own terms
and privacy documentation. Static hosting and network providers may retain
standard access and security logs.

6. COOKIES
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The app does not use advertising cookies. IndexedDB and localStorage are used
for application state, saved workspaces, consent choice, and offline support.
Google Authentication and Firebase may use essential browser storage needed
for authentication, security, and service delivery.

7. CHILDREN'S PRIVACY
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
FET Web is designed for educational institutions and administrators.
We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

8. YOUR RIGHTS
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
You have complete control over your data:
- View: All data is accessible through the application interface
- Export: Download your data at any time
- Delete: Clear all data using the Reset function or browser settings
- Portability: Export and import data in standard formats
- Withdraw analytics consent: Disable anonymous usage analytics in Settings
- Account deletion: Permanently remove cloud data and the sign-in identity

9. CHANGES TO THIS POLICY
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Changes will be reflected
in the "Last Updated" date above.

10. CONTACT
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
FET Web is open-source software based on FET (Free Educational Timetabling).
For questions about this privacy policy, please visit the project repository.
